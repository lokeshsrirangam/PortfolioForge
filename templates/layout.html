About Meridian

Meridian is a quantitative research workspace for portfolio exposure analysis and historical strategy experiments.

  • Strategy Lab runs a long-only moving-average crossover on uploaded close prices.
  • Signals use next-bar execution and include a configurable turnover cost.
  • Portfolio risk scores are heuristic asset-class weights, not forecasts.
  • Historical performance depends on the quality and coverage of the uploaded data.

No live market data is connected. This tool is for research and demonstration, not investment advice.
